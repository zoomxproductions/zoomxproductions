About Zoom-X

Productionwithout boxes.

Zoom-X Productions sits between media production and digital execution. That means a story can begin on camera and continue through a website, an app or a campaign.

01 / APPROACH

The medium follows the idea.

Some projects need a camera. Some need reporting. Some need code. Some need all three. Zoom-X is built to move between those formats without forcing every client through the same template.

02 / STANDARD

Real work over decoration.

The goal is not to make a website look busy or make a video feel expensive. The goal is to make the work clear, credible and useful to the audience it is meant for.

03 / PROCESS

Small enough to stay direct.

Projects begin with the actual need, scope and audience. Communication stays direct, and specialists can be brought into the production when the project requires them.

04 / BASE

Orlando based. Not Orlando limited.

Zoom-X can work with local clients and remote projects. Digital work ships online, and production scope is discussed around location and logistics.

Bring us the project.

Pricing is quoted around the actual scope. Tell us what you need and we will build the right proposal.
