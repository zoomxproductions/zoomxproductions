# Zoom-X Productions — Research and Design Direction

October 2026

## The actual problem

A conventional one-page "creative agency" theme was the wrong reference. Zoom-X is a production/media company with additional digital development and marketing services. It needs to feel like a studio that makes things, not a slide deck of generic service promises. The design should use the supplied Zoom-X logo, its silver-gold geometry and restrained palette, while presenting the work and access to real services clearly.

## Reference patterns observed

- **Koneksyon — https://koneksyondatingapp.com**: clear editorial headings, real product imagery, direct app-store calls to action, and straightforward explanations. Borrow the clarity and intentional spacing, not its one-product landing-page structure.
- **Pentagram — https://www.pentagram.com**: top-level Work, About and Contact are clear, while the portfolio demonstrates the breadth of disciplines and sectors. Give the work its own navigable page.
- **RSA Films — https://rsafilms.com**: filmed work, directors and photographers are prominent; visitors understand the craft through project examples. Zoom-X should eventually have an approved showreel and still gallery.
- **Anonymous Content — https://www.anonymouscontent.com**: project titles and editorial context carry the homepage, with separate pages for talent, production areas and company information. Use direct naming and avoid hollow statistics.
- **UNIT9 — https://www.unit9.com**: Film, Interactive and experiential disciplines are distinct enough to navigate. Zoom-X likewise should not squeeze Journalism, Production, Websites/Apps and Marketing into one repeated card grid.

## Information architecture implemented

1. **Home** — identity, strong type, direct work/service gateways.
2. **Work** — projects and external references with verified destinations when available.
3. **Services** — overview, then individual pages for Video/Photography, Journalism/Media, Websites/Apps, and Marketing.
4. **About** — production philosophy and working model; no fabricated founder story.
5. **Contact** — custom-price inquiry with direct email handoff.
6. **Koneksyon case study** — real digital product example with site and app-store links.

Ten pages total.

## Visual language

- Warm paper / chalk background rather than fake glossy gradient.
- Near-black typography and a touch of gold only where it helps hierarchy.
- Oversized but disciplined sans-serif headlines; editorial serif supporting text.
- Strong horizontal rules, meaningful white space and only minimal directional glyphs.
- Work-first content, not a wall of emoji, neon cards, counters or fake testimonials.
- Original supplied Zoom-X logo, not a reimagined AI mark.
- Direct links to app storefronts for the Koneksyon reference.

## Three-language behavior

English, French and Spanish use separate dictionaries sharing identical keys. Navigation, service headings, explanations and inquiry wording change together; language selection remains in effect across pages where browser storage is available. Text remains readable in English even when JavaScript is unavailable.

## Portfolio verification and limits

Current verifiable links include Koneksyon, Becoming EDU, Greg Promo HT and EDERITO. Wildaina Charles, Akhla Wear and USOUTT could not be accessed reliably by the research browser; Belle Créole Kwizin redirects to a password page. These references remain because the project owner requested them, but they should be checked before launch. No individual websites are claimed as externally commissioned Zoom-X client work without confirming the credits.

## Authentic media required before final public presentation

- Approved film stills / showreel footage from Zoom-X projects.
- Approved professional photography samples.
- Short journalism/media clips or articles with publishing rights.
- Confirmed company introduction, owner/team details and official social links.

Using anonymous stock photos or invented portfolio reels would make the site less credible. The site currently prioritizes truthful digital project references over fabricated footage.

## Release notes

This is a static site designed to be deployed on GitHub Pages, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel, or another static host. The domain and a server-side form backend are not connected by the code alone. The contact form currently prepares an email addressed to info@zoomxproductions.com, without transmitting the form to a server.
